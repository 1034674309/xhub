# Provider catalog and capabilities

[简体中文](../zh-CN/provider-catalog.md) · [Developer documentation](README.md)

Registration describes credentials, discovery, protocols, and transports, not live service availability. Discovery needs declared support and valid credentials. Choose catalog prices explicitly; name matching does not prove price or capability. Unknown/retired providers fail. Test authentication, timeout, malformed responses, and unsupported discovery separately.
